FAQ

The real questions.

Is my data safe?

Your Pat runs on your own server, with your own accounts. Nothing is shared between clients. We only use AI providers where training on your data is turned off (Claude, ChatGPT, Kimi). The AI providers do, of course, process the text sent to them, but they don’t use it to train their models.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

Sometimes it does: that’s why there’s an accountable person. Nothing goes out without your approval, Pat cites its sources, a second AI reviews important work, and I review everything legal, financial or client-facing. Every mistake becomes a permanent rule.

I’m not technical.

That’s exactly what the Pat Managed plan is for: you talk to me, I run Pat. You can also use it directly from the iPhone or Android app, the web, WhatsApp or email.

What if something happens to you?

Your Pat keeps running: it’s yours. Nicolas and Pauline, who already work with Pat, can take over support thanks to sealed emergency access and a written hand-over guide.

Who do I sign with?

UshAIA LLC, a US company registered in Utah. One contract and one invoice, in US dollars, wherever your business is.

Can I stop?

Yes, with 30 days’ notice. You keep your data, your configuration and your apps, and you can come back whenever you like.

How much does the AI itself cost?

You take out your own subscription (for example Claude or ChatGPT, $20 to $200/month depending on use), billed directly by the provider. Pat makes sure you use every dollar of it.

Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude?

If general help is all you need, start there. Pat uses those same models, but connected to your business, on your server, with several providers and a person accountable for the result.
